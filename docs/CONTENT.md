## 14. Copy tone

Use:

- clear international English;
- confident but not inflated claims;
- short paragraphs;
- student-friendly verbs: discover, connect, explore, experience, build;
- institutionally appropriate language.

Avoid:

- "world-class" unless officially approved;
- "the biggest";
- "guaranteed scholarship";
- unverified country counts;
- unverified speaker claims;
- excessive exclamation marks;
- slang on the public website.
